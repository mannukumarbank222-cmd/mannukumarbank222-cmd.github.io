PlayRang – Privacy Policy

App: PlayRang (com.playrang.player) · Developer: Mannu Kumar, Begusarai, Bihar, India · Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the PlayRang app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using PlayRang you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, username, email address and profile photo when you create an account or sign in (including sign-in with Google).
  • Content you create: videos, reels, photos, stories, posts, comments, likes, notes, live streams and channel details you choose to share.
  • Messages: chats, status updates and calls you make in BaatRang, so they can be delivered to the people you choose.
  • AI assistant (RangSaathi): questions you type or speak to RangSaathi. Questions other than in-app help may be sent to Google Gemini to generate an answer. Your password is never sent.
  • Device permissions: camera and microphone (to record videos, photos, voice and live streams), and storage or media access (to upload files and to save videos/MP3 to your offline library). These are used only when you use those features.
  • Basic technical data: app version, device model and crash or error information, used to keep the app working.

2. How we use information

  • To run your account and show your content to the audience you choose.
  • To deliver messages, notifications, followers and activity.
  • To answer your questions through RangSaathi.
  • To keep the platform safe, remove content that breaks our rules (obscene, violent or copied content is not allowed), and fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show third-party advertising based on your personal data.

3. Sharing

Content you post publicly can be seen by other PlayRang users. We share data only with service providers that help us run the app (secure cloud hosting and storage, Google sign-in, and Google Gemini for AI answers), or when the law requires it.

4. Storage and security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Videos saved to your offline library stay on your own phone.

5. Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your posts and profile at any time inside the app.
  • You can turn off camera, microphone or storage permissions in your phone settings.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and its data by emailing us. We will do it within 30 days.

6. Children

PlayRang is not meant for children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
